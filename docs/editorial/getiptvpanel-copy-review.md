# GetIPTVPanel UK Copy Review

## Diagnosis | High confidence | High severity

The rebuild is safer than the former French site, but the priority pages are not ready to compete or convert.

The principal problem is not grammar. It is that the site repeatedly explains caution while under-explaining the product. The commercial pages use abstract headlines, very short supporting copy, repeated pricing language and WhatsApp-only calls to action. A visitor still cannot clearly see the panel, understand why seven panel options differ, or decide which package fits their operation.

## Launch blockers found in the review package

1. **Conflicting WhatsApp configuration**
   - Rendered page extracts use `+212782389820`.
   - `sitewide-copy.md` uses placeholder `+447000000000`.
   - Select one verified number in central configuration and rebuild every link.

2. **Broken or unresolved CTAs**
   - Homepage primary CTA: `Discuss this estimate on WhatsApp -> null`.
   - Become-a-reseller hero buttons: `Prepare application summary -> null` and `Copy summary -> null`.
   - These are P0 conversion defects.

3. **The B2C page defeats the agreed hybrid strategy**
   - `/individual-subscription/` is noindex and tells visitors only that the company focuses on reseller panels.
   - Keep it noindex until the individual offer and rights are verified, but prepare a useful availability page behind the feature gate.

4. **Pricing and tax language needs correction**
   - The repeated statement “No added taxes” should not be published until the business tax treatment and buyer-facing price obligations are confirmed.
   - Safer wording: “No separate panel or setup fee is added to the published credit price. Any applicable tax must be confirmed in the written quote.”

5. **Legal identity is incomplete**
   - Privacy policy is missing the data-controller entity name.
   - Terms are missing the registered business address.
   - Direct checkout should remain disabled until required business and consumer terms are complete.

6. **The UK legality page is correctly noindex**
   - Keep it noindex until a named qualified reviewer checks the final text and official sources.
   - Its current commercial trust strip is inappropriate on a legal explainer and should be removed.

## Sitewide copy diagnosis

### What is working

- UK English and a B2B-first structure are present.
- Keyword-to-page separation is mostly sensible.
- The site avoids fake reviews, broadcaster logos, sideloading instructions and guaranteed earnings.
- The package/credit figures are centrally configured.
- The legal distinction between panel access and content rights is explicit.
- Device guides use official-store routes.

### What needs refinement

- The exact phrase “GetIPTVPanel UK Reseller Platform” appears as the eyebrow on nearly every page and adds little value.
- The same trust text about £12 credits appears on informational, legal and setup pages where it is irrelevant.
- Several H1s are clever but do not state the target topic plainly.
- Commercial pages are too thin and lack concrete panel evidence.
- The homepage leads with a profit estimate before fully explaining the offer.
- There are no visible real panel screenshots, demonstrations, case studies or operating proof.
- The seven panel options are referenced but not differentiated in extracted copy.
- WhatsApp is the only substantive conversion route.
- The support and company identity signals do not yet match a UK-first positioning.
- The footer claim “UK B2B reseller account management software” must match the actual product and contracting entity.

## Priority editorial changes

### Homepage

Replace the generic H1 with a direct keyword and product statement. Explain what the panel does before discussing margins. Move the calculator below product workflow and packages.

### Reseller panel

Add a real feature matrix and a labelled walkthrough. Explain the account lifecycle, credit ledger, renewals, customer references, permissions, data boundaries and support escalation. Do not imply controls that the panel does not have.

### Packages

Show the actual seven configured panel names. Group them by verified unit rate. Explain minimum order, credit definition, quote validity, larger-order pricing, renewal and refund rules. Never hide essential terms behind WhatsApp.

### Become a reseller

Keep the qualification approach, but add a complete step-by-step operating plan and explain what happens after the application. Avoid “business opportunity” language that implies easy income.

### Profit calculator

Rename outputs to revenue, gross margin and estimated operating contribution. Add a break-even result and unsold-credit warning. Do not label the result net profit.

### Individual subscription

Prepare a real B2C page, but keep it noindex and non-purchasable until the package, territory, rights, price, renewal, refund and fulfilment data are verified. The current reseller-only copy wastes B2C demand.

### Buyer guide

Expand the checklist with practical examples and a two-route CTA: individual availability or reseller packages. Do not claim to rank or review providers without a documented methodology.

### UK legality page

Remove sales pricing from the trust strip. Link directly to current official UK copyright and illicit-streaming guidance. Retain noindex until legal review.

### Device guides

The guides are the strongest current content. Improve the H1s for direct search intent, add screenshots and clarify which support team handles player issues versus provider-account issues.

## Evidence required before adding stronger copy

- Exact names and differences of all seven panels.
- Real panel screenshots or a labelled demonstration.
- Verified support hours and response method.
- Contracting entity and business address.
- Tax/VAT treatment.
- Credit expiry and refund rules.
- Written evidence for any individual subscription offer.
- Verified UK-facing telephone or WhatsApp number.
- Payment method and billing descriptor.
- Real customer evidence, only if consented and verifiable.

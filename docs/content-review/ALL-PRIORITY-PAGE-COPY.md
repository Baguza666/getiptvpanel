## Accordions and FAQs

#### Q1: What does one reseller credit activate?
**Answer**: One credit activates one 12-month account under the applicable panel terms. Confirm the exact account scope and any restrictions in the written quote.

#### Q2: What is the minimum reseller order?
**Answer**: The published minimum is 10 credits. A different quote may apply to larger orders.

#### Q3: Does panel access include content distribution rights?
**Answer**: No. Buying panel access or credits does not grant rights to distribute third-party programmes, channels, films, sports or other protected content. The operator remains responsible for holding the permissions required in every territory served.

#### Q4: Are earnings guaranteed?
**Answer**: No. Revenue, margin and customer retention depend on your selling price, operating costs, lawful offer, support quality and demand. The calculator is an estimate only.

#### Q5: How do I choose between the panel options?
**Answer**: Compare the verified features, rate, minimum order, customer workflow, support route and complete written terms. Do not choose on unit price alone.

### Section 2: The practical distinction

Lawful services have permission to supply the relevant material in the relevant territory. That permission may come from ownership, a licence or another valid legal basis.

Problems arise when copyrighted programmes, films, broadcasts or recordings are copied, communicated, supplied or resold without permission and no legal exception applies.

UK Government guidance states that using copyright material generally requires permission, a licence, ownership of the rights or an applicable exception.

### Section 3: What a buyer should check

Ask the supplier to identify:

Contracting businessExact packageTerritory coveredRights owner or licensorPermission to distributePermission to resell, when relevantDuration of the permissionSupported devicesPrice, renewal and refund terms

A player app, playlist format, panel login or payment receipt does not by itself establish that permission exists.

### Section 4: What a reseller should check

A reseller should obtain written confirmation that the upstream supplier may supply the relevant package and that resale is permitted in the intended territory.

Panel access does not create those rights. When the evidence is unclear, do not market or resell the package until qualified legal advice has been obtained.
